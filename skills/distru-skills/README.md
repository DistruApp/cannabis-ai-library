# Distru Skills

Skills that work with your live Distru data. Connect your Distru account to your agent (the Distru MCP) before you use them. They read Distru; none of them change Distru records or send anything for you.

- **[distru-expiring-inventory](./distru-expiring-inventory/SKILL.md)**: Build, review or customize a dashboard of stock about to expire, the customers who already buy it, and a promo email brief per buyer that your agent drafts for you to review. Works in Claude (artifacts, Claude Code) and Codex / ChatGPT (Sites, Codex app and CLI).
