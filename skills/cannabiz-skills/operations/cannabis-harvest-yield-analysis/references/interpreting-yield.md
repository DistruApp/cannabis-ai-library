# Interpreting yield metrics: what to check when a number looks off

Operational checks only. No outside benchmarks: compare against the operator's own batches or targets.

| Signal | Check first (data/process) | Then (cultivation) |
|---|---|---|
| Dry-down much higher than the operator's other batches | Wet weight taken with stems/fan leaves this time but not others; dry weight taken before the cure finished; unit mix-up (lb entered as g) | Harvest timing, environment in dry room |
| Dry-down much lower | Dry weight taken too early (product still wet); wet weight taken after partial drying | Dry-room humidity/temperature, timing of weigh |
| Low g per sq ft, normal g per plant | Canopy figure includes aisles or empty benches; plant density differs | Plant spacing, canopy fill |
| Low g per plant, normal g per sq ft | Plant count includes culled or dead plants | Plant density too high |
| High trim % | Machine vs. hand trim changed; smalls classified as trim | Genetics, defoliation, larf |
| Unaccounted weight | Waste not logged, QA/lab samples not recorded, a bin weighed twice or not at all, breakdown taken from a different weigh event | — |
| Cost per gram spike | One-time cost charged to one batch; small batch carrying fixed room cost | Low yield in that batch |

When strain and room are the same thing in the data (one strain per room), say the effect can't be separated and suggest rotating a strain into another room if the grower wants to know.
