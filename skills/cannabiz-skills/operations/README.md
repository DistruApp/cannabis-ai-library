# Operations

Cost, yield, purchasing and staffing.

- **[cannabis-batch-cogs](./cannabis-batch-cogs/SKILL.md)**: Calculate the true cost of a manufacturing batch from its BOM, labor, overhead and testing, with cost per unit, yield variance and a sensitivity table.
- **[cannabis-harvest-yield-analysis](./cannabis-harvest-yield-analysis/SKILL.md)**: Analyze harvests by batch, strain and room: dry-down, yield per plant, square foot and light, flower and trim mix, and cost per gram.
- **[cannabis-replenishment-planner](./cannabis-replenishment-planner/SKILL.md)**: Turn sales and on-hand exports into a reorder plan with days of supply, stockout and dead stock flags, and a draft PO by vendor.
- **[staff-scheduling-optimizer](./staff-scheduling-optimizer/SKILL.md)**: Recommend staffing by shift from real demand, a labor budget and availability. Flags overtime risk, coverage gaps and agent card expirations.
