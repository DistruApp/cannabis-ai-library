# State profiles

**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-11 (independent fact-check of all pilot states). **Review again by:** 2027-02-09 (90 days), or sooner if a state announces a rule or system change. Several official sites block automated access (see **Blocked sites** below), so some items rest on the Cornell LII copy of the rules and are labelled V (LII) or NEEDS VERIFICATION.

How to read this file:
- **V** = confirmed on the regulator's own site, PDF or form on the review date.
- **V (LII)** = text confirmed on the Cornell LII copy of the regulation (a secondary source) on the review date; the regulator's own site was not checked.
- **V\*** = quote confirmed directly by the library maintainer, not only by the research pass.
- **Vendor-only** = confirmed only on the vendor's own site.
- **NEEDS VERIFICATION** = the text was confirmed, but the copy checked may predate the current rules. Confirm the current version before relying on it.
- **UNVERIFIED** = don't rely on it. Seen only on an unapproved secondary source (such as FindLaw or a search snippet), or the source couldn't be reached. Ask the operator or check the source.

A rule listed here is a **pointer, not legal advice**. Always give the citation and tell the operator to confirm the current text, especially before submitting anything.

**Never carry a threshold or deadline across states.** Where a state section says it defines no numeric significant-discrepancy threshold, say so, and don't apply another state's 2% or 5%.

Each pilot state's rules are grouped under the same headings: **Track and trace**, **Reconciliation and discrepancies**, **Notifications**, **Adjustments and corrections**, **Transfers, manifests and testing**, **Records retention**, **Waste and destruction**, **Worker credentials**.

## Pilot states (deep coverage)

### California (CA)
- **Regulator:** Department of Cannabis Control (DCC). https://cannabis.ca.gov/ (V)
- **Rules:** 4 CCR Division 19. https://www.cannabis.ca.gov/cannabis-laws/dcc-regulations/ The DCC's text page is an *unofficial* compilation "in effect as of October 1, 2026". The official text is in the CCR on Westlaw. Items marked V (LII) were checked on the Cornell LII copy (https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/california/4-CCR-<section>, history notes to 2022) on 2026-11-11.
- **Traceability:** Metrc (California Cannabis Track-and-Trace, CCTT) (V). Source: https://cannabis.ca.gov/licensees/cannaconnect-compliance-hub/
- **Licensee lookup:** https://search.cannabis.ca.gov (linked from DCC; not opened)
- Quotes marked V\* were confirmed on 2026-10-16 from the DCC compilation. The §15049(b), §15049.1, §15051, §15034, §15036(a) and §15048.1(a)(4),(8) quotes were also found verbatim on the Cornell LII copy on 2026-11-11.

#### Track and trace
- **§15049(b), 24-hour recording (V\*):** "Each of the following activities shall be recorded in the track and trace system within 24 hours of occurrence". The listed activities include receipt, rejection of transfers, manufacturing, internal QC/R&D use, destruction or disposal, packaging/repackaging (cultivators: see §15049.1(b)(5)), lab testing, and sale or donation.
- **§15049.1, cultivation (V\*):** certain cultivation activities must be recorded "within three (3) calendar days of occurrence". See the section for the list.

#### Reconciliation and discrepancies
- **§15051, Reconciliation (V\*):** review the track and trace records "at least once every 30 calendar days", including "Reconciling on-hand inventory … with the records in the track and trace system". If a discrepancy is found, "the licensee shall conduct an audit and notify the Department in writing if the discrepancy is significant as defined in section 15034."
- **§15048.1, Account manager (V\*):** reconcile "at least once every thirty (30) calendar days."
- **§15034, Significant discrepancy (V\*):** "a five percent difference between the licensee's physical inventory and the inventory recorded in the track and trace system." *The text doesn't say whether 5% is measured per package, per item, or on total inventory, or by weight or by count. Present both readings and have compliance or counsel decide. Don't pick one.*

#### Notifications
- **§15036(a)(1), significant discrepancy: DCC and local law enforcement within 24 hours, on form DCC-LIC-028:** "A licensee shall notify the Department and local law enforcement within 24 hours of discovery" when "The licensee discovers a significant discrepancy, as defined in section 15034, in its inventory." §15036(b): the notice to DCC goes on form DCC-LIC-028 (Notifications and Requests Regarding Regulatory Compliance) and includes the date and time, the local law enforcement agency notified, and a description, including the items involved. The same section also covers diversion, theft, loss, criminal activity, loss or alteration of records, and security breaches. (Rule text V (LII), checked 2026-11-10 and 2026-11-11, Cornell copy amended 2022-11-07. Form V, DCC's own DCC-LIC-028 PDF dated January 2026; that's a form revision date, so check it's still the current form.) **This is a separate, faster deadline than the §15051 written notice. Always state both when a significant discrepancy is possible.**
- **§15051, written notice after an audit:** see Reconciliation and discrepancies above.
- **§15048.1(a)(7) / §15050, loss of track-and-trace access:** "Notify the Department of any loss of access to the track and trace system that exceeds 72 hours". While access is down, don't transport, receive or deliver, and keep paper records. Once restored, enter the activity "Within three calendar days". (V (LII))

#### Adjustments and corrections
- **§15049(d), adjustment reason:** for package adjustments, "the licensee shall include a description explaining the reason for adjustment." (V\* on 2026-10-16, where the DCC compilation was cited as §15049(e). On the Cornell LII copy this text is at (d), and (e) is partial-shipment rejection. Cite (d) unless the current DCC compilation numbers it (e), and say which source you cited.)
- **§15048.1(a)(4), corrections:** "Correct any data entry errors within three (3) calendar days of discovery of the error" (V\*; also V (LII)).

#### Transfers, manifests and testing
- **§15049.2(a), shipping manifest (in T&T, before transfer):** must include the origin, distributor and receiver (name, license number, premises address), "The UID numbers for all items", item name, category and weight or count per tag, estimated departure and arrival, driver's license number, and vehicle make, model and plate. §15049.2(d): "If there are any discrepancies … the licensee shall reject the shipment." §15314(b): the manifest goes to DCC and the receiver before transport. (V (LII))
- **Testing:** in CA, regulatory testing happens at the distributor (§15304), so **cultivator→distributor transfers of untested product are normal and shouldn't be held.** Before transporting to a retailer, the distributor must confirm (§15307) that the COA matches the batch and is "less than 12 months old", that labels match the COA, and that the T&T weight or count matches. (V (LII))

#### Records retention
- **§15037(a):** "Records must be kept for at least seven years from the date of creation, unless a shorter time is specified." (V (LII))

#### Waste and destruction
- **§17223, waste plan:** a written cannabis waste management plan is required (quoted by the research pass, V). §17223 sits in the cultivation chapter (Ch. 4), and requires waste to be kept in a secured receptacle or area until disposal (§17223(c)). (V (LII))
- **§15049(b)(5), (c)(6), recording destruction:** record it within 24 h. The notes must give "(A) The name of the employee performing the destruction or disposal; (B) The reason for destruction or disposal; and (C) The method of disposal." No holding period, witness or video rule was found. (V (LII))

#### Worker credentials
- **§15043, badges:** staff of retailers and temporary events must display a licensee-issued "laminated or plastic-coated identification badge" (DBA, license number, first name, employee number, color photo). There's no state-issued worker credential. (V (LII))

- **Pending change:** DCC rulemaking DCC-2026-02-R may amend these sections. Text and status are UNVERIFIED.

### Colorado (CO)
- **Regulator:** Marijuana Enforcement Division (MED). MED's pages have moved from https://med.colorado.gov/ to https://sbg.colorado.gov/med. Both blocked automated access on 2026-11-11, so older med.colorado.gov links below may redirect or break (see **Blocked sites**).
- **Rules:** 1 CCR 212-3. https://sbg.colorado.gov/med/rules (formerly https://med.colorado.gov/rules). Earlier reviews listed revised rules adopted 2026-09-10 and effective 2026-11-01. That date is UNVERIFIED: the official sites are blocked, and a web search found only a 2026-01-05 rulemaking.
- **Every CO rule quote below is from the Cornell LII copy of 1 CCR 212-3, amended through 2024-12-04** (https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/colorado/1-CCR-212-3-<rule>), so each is **V (LII), NEEDS VERIFICATION** against the January 2026 rules and any later version.
- **Traceability:** Metrc. MED's Metrc page said licensees are "required to use METRC as the primary inventory tracking system of record" (V on 2026-10-16, https://med.colorado.gov/metrc; couldn't be re-fetched on 2026-11-11).
- **Licensee lookup:** https://med.colorado.gov/licensee-information-and-lookup-tool (V on 2026-10-16; the site has since moved, so the link may have changed)

#### Track and trace
- **Rule 3-805(A):** "Licensees are required to use the Inventory Tracking System as the primary inventory tracking system of record." No hour-based entry deadline was found; entries are governed by daily reconciliation. **NEEDS VERIFICATION.**
- **Rule 3-805(E)(4):** "Secondary systems must use the Inventory Tracking System data as the primary source of data and must be compatible with updating to the Inventory Tracking System." This matters for ERP/POS: Metrc is the record of truth. **NEEDS VERIFICATION.**

#### Reconciliation and discrepancies
- **Rule 3-805(E)(1):** "A Licensee must reconcile all on-premises and in-transit Regulated Marijuana inventories each day in the Inventory Tracking System at the close of business." (The same wording appeared in the Secretary of State's 1 CCR 212-3 text retrieved 2026-11-10, but which version that was wasn't confirmed.) **NEEDS VERIFICATION.**
- **No significant-discrepancy threshold.** CO rules define no numeric significant-discrepancy threshold (none found in 3-805, 3-905 or 3-920). Don't apply CA's 5% or any other state's threshold.

#### Notifications
- **Rule 3-920(B), crime, diversion or ITS compromise: the Division within 14 days:** report to the Division any discovered plan or action to commit "theft, burglary, underage sales, diversion of marijuana or marijuana product, or other crime", or to "compromise the integrity of the Inventory Tracking System", "as soon as possible after the discovery of the action, but not later than 14 days". This "does not alter or eliminate" any duty to report to local law enforcement. **NEEDS VERIFICATION.**
- **Rule 3-920(C), (D):** adverse health events to the Division within 48 h; fire to the Division within 48 h. **NEEDS VERIFICATION.**
- **Rule 3-905(B)(24):** the security plan must include theft-reporting and post-theft reconciliation protocols. No notification form is named in the rule text. **NEEDS VERIFICATION.**

#### Adjustments and corrections
- **Rule 3-805(I)(6):** ITS entries must include "a note explaining the reason for any destruction of Regulated Marijuana, and reason for any adjustment of weights to Inventory Tracking System packages." 3-605(I)(3): receipt discrepancies "shall be made in the Inventory Tracking System". 3-805(F)(3): after a loss of ITS access, no transfers until access is restored and back-entered. No correction-deadline rule was found. **NEEDS VERIFICATION.**

#### Transfers, manifests and testing
- **Rule 3-605(H)(3), manifest:** departure date and time; origin and destination name, address and license; transporter (if any); product name and quantity (by weight and unit) per destination; arrival date and ETA; vehicle make, model and plate; and the name and license number of the escorting licensee. A copy goes to each recipient (3-605(H)(2)). During transport, carry the business license, the driver's Owner or Employee License, a driver's license, and registration and insurance (3-605(E)). **NEEDS VERIFICATION.**
- **Testing:** "shall not Transfer or process … unless Test Batches … has been tested … and passed all contaminant tests" (4-215(A)(1); exceptions are 5-205(C), 6-205(C) and Reduced Testing Allowance). Potency testing is required before transfer (4-210). **NEEDS VERIFICATION.**

#### Records retention
- **Rule 3-905:** keep six months of records on premises. The listed records are kept for the current and preceding calendar year. Books and records "necessary to fully account for the business transactions" are kept for the "current year and three immediate prior tax years". Off-premises records must be produced within 3 business days of a request. **NEEDS VERIFICATION.**

#### Waste and destruction
- **Rule 3-230:** waste must be made "unusable and Unrecognizable prior to leaving the Licensed Premises" (≥50% non-marijuana mix, or compost, digestion, biochar or gasification). It must be weighed and tracked in the ITS, and a waste log kept. Third-party mobile rendering must be recorded on video (3-230(F)(4)). No advance-notice or holding period was found in this version. **NEEDS VERIFICATION.**

#### Worker credentials
- **Rule 3-205(A):** everyone in a Limited Access Area must "hold and properly display a current Identification Badge issued by the Division" (Owner or Employee License), worn visibly at or above the waist. **NEEDS VERIFICATION.**

### Michigan (MI)
- **Regulator:** Cannabis Regulatory Agency (CRA). https://www.michigan.gov/cra. The site blocked automated access, so it's UNVERIFIED by fetch. Check in a browser (see **Blocked sites**).
- **Traceability:** Metrc, the "statewide monitoring system" (Vendor-only: https://www.metrc.com/partner/michigan/). Latest MI Metrc bulletin seen: Bulletin 118 (2026-10-14).
- **Rules:** Mich. Admin. Code R 420, read on the Cornell LII copy (https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/michigan/Mich-Admin-Code-R-420-<n>; most rules eff. 2022-03-07, R 420.804 eff. 2020-06-22). The statute (Marihuana Tracking Act, MCL 333.27903), CRA's own rules pages and CRA bulletins couldn't be reached. The claim that a CRA rules rewrite is pending is UNVERIFIED. **Every MI rule below is V (LII), NEEDS VERIFICATION** against CRA's current rules.

#### Track and trace
- **R 420.210:** "A licensee shall not transfer or sell a marihuana product that is not identified in the statewide monitoring system … A licensee shall immediately tag, identify, or record as part of a batch…".

#### Reconciliation and discrepancies
- No reconciliation frequency was found in R 420 (the statute and bulletins were blocked).
- **No significant-discrepancy threshold.** MI rules set no numeric significant-discrepancy threshold (none found in R 420). Don't apply CA's 5% or any other state's threshold. Treat an unexplained shortage as possible "loss" and escalate to compliance the same day (R 420.804, below).

#### Notifications
- **R 420.804(1), theft or loss: the agency and local law enforcement within 24 hours:** "Licensees shall notify the agency and local law enforcement authorities within 24 hours of becoming aware of, or within 24 hours of when the licensee should have been aware of, the theft or loss of any marihuana product or criminal activity at the marihuana business."
- **R 420.802:** employee or third-party violations, 3 business days; fire, 1 business day (agency and BFS).
- **R 420.602(2)(a):** an employee's felony charge or conviction must be reported "immediately".

#### Adjustments and corrections
- No adjustment-reason or correction-deadline rule was found in R 420. CRA and Metrc bulletins were blocked. Get adjustment reasons from the operator's Metrc account.

#### Transfers, manifests and testing
- **Testing before transfer:** a cultivator can't transfer or sell to a sales location until "All test results must indicate passed in the statewide monitoring system" (R 420.303(5)). **Exception:** untested product may go to a producer for processing (R 420.303(6)). Quarantined product "may not be packaged, transferred, or sold until passing test results are entered" (R 420.304(1)(k)).
- **Holds and recalls:** before any sale or transfer, verify in Metrc that the product isn't on administrative hold, recalled, or ordered destroyed (R 420.502(5)).
- **Manifest:** transporters must carry a route plan and manifest, must not possess product that isn't on the manifest, must wait on site until the product is weighed and accepted or rejected, and may hold custody for no more than 96 h (R 420.206(7)(e)-(j)).

#### Records retention
- **R 420.203(2)(f)(iii):** "Licensee records must be maintained for at least 4 years", or longer if an investigation is open.

#### Waste and destruction
- **R 420.502(4),(6), 90-day destruction deadline:** expired product can't be sold or transferred and "must be destroyed". "A marihuana business shall destroy all product required to be destroyed for any reason within 90 calendar days of when the marihuana business became aware".
- **R 420.211(1),(4),(8):** waste must be made unusable and unrecognizable (≥50% non-marihuana mix), "recorded in the statewide monitoring system", and logged.

#### Worker credentials
- There's no state worker badge or employee license. Employees must be entered in Metrc "within 7 business days of hiring", and removed within 7 business days of termination (R 420.602(2)(b)-(c)).

### Massachusetts (MA)
- **Regulator:** Cannabis Control Commission (CCC). https://masscannabiscontrol.com/ (V)
- **Rules:** 935 CMR 500 (adult use) and 501 (medical), effective 2026-09-11. https://masscannabiscontrol.com/regulations/ The 935 CMR 500 quotes below are from the CCC's own PDF "935 CMR 500.000 … Effective 09-11-2026" (self-described as an unofficial compilation), https://masscannabiscontrol.com/document/935-cmr-500-000-adult-use-of-marijuana-effective-09-11-26-pdf/, checked 2026-11-11 (V). 935 CMR 501 (medical) was checked on the Cornell LII copy, history to 2024-11-22 (V (LII)).
- **Traceability:** Metrc (V). https://masscannabiscontrol.com/metrc/
- **Licensee lookup:** https://masscannabiscontrol.com/licensing-tracker/
- **Guidance:** CCC "Massachusetts Seed-to-Sale Guidance", May 2021, https://masscannabiscontrol.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/20210517_Guidance_Seed-to-Sale.pdf. It's guidance, not regulation, and dated 2021, so check it's still current. Items from it are labelled "Guidance V, 2021".

#### Track and trace
- **935 CMR 500.105(8):** "Real-time inventory shall be maintained", and "failure to enter inventory into the Seed-to-sale SOR may result in the suspension or revocation". (V)
- **Guidance:** "at the end of day physical inventory must match Metrc". Waste must be "accounted for by the end of the business day". (Guidance V, 2021.)

#### Reconciliation and discrepancies
- **935 CMR 500.105(8):** "Conduct a monthly inventory … Conduct a comprehensive annual inventory". (V)
- **Any discrepancy is reportable.** There is no % threshold: 500.110(9) lists "Discovery of inventory discrepancies" as a reportable incident, so every discrepancy triggers the 24-hour notice below. Don't apply CA's 5% or any other state's threshold.

#### Notifications
- **935 CMR 500.110(9), incident reporting: law enforcement and the CCC within 24 hours, then a report within 10 days:** notify "appropriate Law Enforcement Authorities and the Commission … immediately and, in no instance, more than 24 hours following discovery". Triggers include "Discovery of inventory discrepancies", diversion, theft or loss, unauthorized destruction, and loss or alteration of records. **There is no % threshold: any discrepancy counts.** Then, "within ten calendar days", submit an incident report to the Commission. Keep the documentation for at least 1 year. Medical MTCs: 501.110(9) has the same deadlines (V (LII)). (V, CCC PDF effective 09-11-2026.) **Always state both deadlines.**
- **935 CMR 500.105(13), transport:** report "any unusual discrepancy in weight, count, or inventory" within 24 hours. (V)
- **935 CMR 500.160, failed testing:** notify the CCC within 72 h of a non-remediable failed result. (V)
- Agent registration card changes: see Worker credentials.

#### Adjustments and corrections
- **Guidance:** a wrong wet weight can be corrected through Metrc Support within 48 h; after that, file an incident report. Retail voids must be reconciled in Metrc and POS within 24 h. QC-sample adjustments need a comment. (Guidance V, 2021.)

#### Transfers, manifests and testing
- **935 CMR 500.105(13)(f), manifest:** completed before transport and sent to the destination beforehand. It includes origin and destination (name, address, registration number), the names and registration numbers of the transporting agents and the preparer, the product description with weight and form, mileage, departure and arrival times, before and after weight or count, the reweigh time, and the receiver's signature. Retain for at least 1 year. (V)
- **Testing:** "No Marijuana Product … may be sold or otherwise marketed for adult use that has not first been tested" (500.105(8)(h), V). CCC guidance (May 2021, Q39): "a Licensee may only transfer cannabis product that has test results. This requirement includes transfers between licenses". Failed product may move only cultivator to manufacturer, for remediation. (Guidance V, 2021.)

#### Records retention
- Waste records 3 years (500.105(12)(d)); manifests at least 1 year (500.105(13)(f)); incident documents at least 1 year or the length of an investigation (500.110(9)); all records 2 years after closure (500.105(9)(g)). (V)

#### Waste and destruction
- **935 CMR 500.105(12)(c)-(d):** grind and mix until unusable. "A Marijuana Establishment Agent shall witness and document" rendering. Keep an electronic record of date, type, quantity, manner, location, and the agent's name and signature, for at least 3 years. **The 2026-09-11 text requires one agent. Earlier versions required "No fewer than two".** Don't use old SOPs. (V)

#### Worker credentials
- **935 CMR 500.030(7):** "A Marijuana Establishment Agent shall always carry a Registration Card" while handling or transporting product. Report changes or a lost or stolen card within 5 business days (500.030(6)). (V)

### New York (NY)
- **Regulator:** Office of Cannabis Management (OCM). https://cannabis.ny.gov/ (V)
- **Rules:** 9 NYCRR Parts 113–133 (linked from OCM). The Part 125 quotes below are from the Cornell LII copy (https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-york/9-NYCRR-125.8, -125.3, -125.10, -125.11, -125.13), eff. 2023-09-27, checked 2026-11-11. **Each is V (LII), NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Part 125 wasn't checked for post-2023 amendments. Confirm on the official NYCRR.
- **Traceability:** **Metrc, which replaced BioTrack NY** (V). https://cannabis.ny.gov/seed-to-sale. OCM's Metrc implementation plan (V, https://cannabis.ny.gov/metrc-nys-implementation-plan, checked 2026-11-11): non-retail licensees had to have "All existing inventory … in the Metrc system" by 2025-12-17; dispensaries by "January 12, 2026 (new date)", when they also had to "start reporting sales to Metrc". From 2026-02-28, finished goods sent from a distributor to a dispensary "MUST have Retail Item ID QR code on each unit". From 2026-03-31 they must also carry "A 'TestPassed' or 'RetestPassed' testing status associated to each package prior to transfer." C.O.D. reporting is not covered by this document and stays UNVERIFIED.
- **Licensee lookup:** https://data.ny.gov/Economic-Development/Current-OCM-Licenses/jskf-tt3q
- **Gotcha:** data and SOPs from the BioTrack era are out of date. Don't apply BioTrack workflows to NY.

#### Track and trace
- **§125.8(a):** "electronic real-time inventory tracking system"; inventory "as well as inventory adjustments" recorded in real time. **§125.8(a)(8):** nursery and cultivation activities "within three (3) calendar days of occurrence".

#### Reconciliation and discrepancies
- **§125.8(d), audits:** "conducting a monthly inventory audit of all cannabis and cannabis products", plus an annual audit.
- **§125.8(e), significant discrepancy:** the threshold is measured against *anticipated inventory* and depends on license type. It is 2% for cooperatives, Tier 3–5 cultivators, ROD and ROND. Retail dispensaries, delivery and on-site consumption use "five (5) percent of the anticipated inventory or greater" when the system anticipated 1,000 products or fewer, and 2% above 1,000. Nurseries, Tier 1–2 cultivators, distributors, processors and microbusinesses use 5% (2% if the licensee also holds a 2%-tier license). The tier list is paraphrased. *The rule doesn't say whether this is measured by weight, count or package. Present the readings; don't pick one.*

#### Notifications
- **§125.8(e), significant discrepancy: OCM within 24 hours:** "shall notify the Office no later than twenty-four (24) hours after discovery of the event", in the manner the Office prescribes.
- **§125.3(e), theft, loss or diversion: OCM within 24 hours, then an incident report within 10 days:** notify OCM "immediately and, in no instance, more than twenty-four (24) hours following discovery" of incidents that include "diversion, theft, or loss of any cannabis or cannabis product". **Separately**, file an incident report "within ten (10) calendar days of any incident" that includes "confirmation that the appropriate law enforcement authorities were notified." Always state both deadlines.

#### Adjustments and corrections
- **§125.8(a):** inventory adjustments are recorded in real time (see Track and trace).
- **§125.8(b)(4), outage:** "Within three (3) calendar days of restored access, enter all licensed cannabis activity". No transfers until access is restored.
- **Transfer variances (OCM STS Bulletin 18, 2026-04-27, V):** if a package is short, the receiver "may accept the full package and adjust the quantity in Metrc using the entry error action reason". "If the variance between the shipped and received quantity exceeds 5%, it will be flagged in Metrc." https://cannabis.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2026/04/sts-bulletin-18.pdf
- No regulatory deadline for correcting data-entry errors was found.

#### Transfers, manifests and testing
- **§125.10(g):** the manifest is generated "Prior to transporting". For product going to retail licensees, "a certificate of analysis shall accompany cannabis products".
- Retail Item ID and TestPassed/RetestPassed requirements for distributor→dispensary transfers: see Traceability above.

#### Records retention
- **§125.13(a):** records are kept "for at least five (5) years from the date of creation". Manifests are kept "for a period of five (5) years" (§125.10(g)). Incident records are kept "not less than five (5) years or the duration of an open investigation, whichever is longer." (§125.3)

#### Waste and destruction
- **§125.11(j):** "All cannabis waste disposed of shall be weighed, recorded, and entered into the inventory tracking system prior to mixing and disposal." Waste is mixed so it is at least 50% non-cannabis, two employees witness, and records are kept 5 years. No advance notice to OCM was found.

#### Worker credentials
- **§125.3(d)(1):** each employee must "hold and properly display an identification badge, issued by the licensee, at all times". There's no state-issued worker permit.

### Illinois (IL)
- **Regulators:** split by license type. IDFPR (dispensaries); IDOA (cultivation centers, craft growers, infusers, transporters); IDPH (medical patients). https://cannabis.illinois.gov/ (V on 2026-10-16; unreachable on 2026-11-11)

#### Track and trace
- **Dispensaries, 1291.310(b)(1):** "Each sales transaction at the time of sale" (V (LII)).
- **IDOA licensees:** recording timing is in the new Subpart K, which is UNVERIFIED (see IDOA rule update).

#### Reconciliation and discrepancies
- **Dispensaries, 1291.310(e):** "An AIC shall conduct a daily physical count of all inventory". Bulk "must be counted monthly". (V (LII)) 410 ILCS 705/15-75(d): "The agent-in-charge shall conduct daily inventory reconciliation." (UNVERIFIED, FindLaw.)
- **Cultivation centers, 8 IAC 1300.180(b):** "a physical weekly inventory of cannabis stock" (V (LII), pre-2026-05-01 text; NEEDS VERIFICATION).
- **No significant-discrepancy threshold.** IL rules define no percentage "significant discrepancy" threshold; they use "imbalance". Don't import another state's 2% or 5%.

#### Notifications
- **Dispensaries (IDFPR), 68 Ill. Adm. Code 1291.310** (V (LII), eff. 2024-08-20; checked 2026-11-11):
  - **(e)(3), theft or criminal activity: IDFPR and ISP in writing within 24 hours:** "Within 24 hours after the first discovery of the reduction due to theft, criminal activity or suspected criminal activity … the dispensing organization shall inform the Department and ISP in writing."
  - **(e)(2), imbalance from a mistake: IDFPR immediately if the cause isn't found in 2 calendar days; investigation within 10 business days:** if it "cannot identify the reason for the mistake within two calendar days", it "shall inform the Department immediately in writing". "The dispensing organization shall complete its investigation within 10 business days."
- **Cultivation centers (IDOA), 410 ILCS 705/20-30(h): local police, ISP and IDOA within 24 hours, by phone or in person** (UNVERIFIED: seen on FindLaw; ilga.gov was unreachable on 2026-11-11): "within 24 hours of the discovery of any loss or theft", notify "local law enforcement, the Illinois State Police, and the Department of Agriculture". "Notification shall be made by phone or in person". Craft-grower and infuser equivalents are UNVERIFIED.
- **Destruction notice:** see Waste and destruction (IDOA licensees: 7 days' notice).

#### Adjustments and corrections
- **Dispensaries, 1291.310(e)(1), Department approval first:** "A dispensing organization must receive Department approval prior to completing an inventory adjustment." It must give "a detailed reason for the adjustment". "Inventory adjustment documentation must be kept at the dispensary for two years from the date performed." (V (LII)) **Don't tell an IL dispensary to post a Metrc adjustment before it has approval.**
- No IDOA adjustment rule was verified.

#### Transfers, manifests and testing
- **Receiving, 1291.310(d):** "Upon cannabis delivery, a dispensing organization shall confirm the product's name" and the other manifest details against the package (V (LII)).
- **Manifest (UNVERIFIED, search-result text only):** 8 IAC 1300.1040 (Subpart K) has the manifest prepared "through the cannabis plant monitoring system, prior to transferring". 410 ILCS 705/40-25(k): a manifest "shall be present in any vehicle transporting cannabis".
- **Testing, 8 IAC 1300.700(e):** "must pass all testing requirements before offered for sale to the public" (V (LII), eff. 2020-06-03; NEEDS VERIFICATION).

#### Records retention
- Dispensary records 5 years (410 ILCS 705/15-75(e)(1), UNVERIFIED, FindLaw); adjustment documents 2 years (1291.310(e)(1), V (LII)); dispensary destruction records "at least five years" (1291.325(f), V (LII)); cultivation 5 years (8 IAC 1300.155(a), V (LII), NEEDS VERIFICATION).

#### Waste and destruction
- **IDOA licensees, 8 Ill. Adm. Code 1300.810(b): 7 days' notice:** "shall provide the Department and ISP a minimum of 7 days' notice prior to rendering the product unusable". IDOA may hold product up to 7 days or send a witness. (V (LII), eff. 2020-06-03. Part 1300 was amended effective 2026-05-01, so NEEDS VERIFICATION against the current text.)
- **Dispensaries, 68 Ill. Adm. Code 1291.325:** the destruction day and time are given to the Department and ISP at the initial inspection, and changes need notice "at least three business days before the implementation". Flower must be "entered into the State Verification System prior to rendering it unusable", "conducted under video surveillance". Records: "at least five years." (V (LII), eff. 2024-08-20.)

#### Worker credentials
- **Dispensaries:** "A dispensing organization agent must keep his or her identification card visible at all times when in the dispensary." (410 ILCS 705/15-40(b), UNVERIFIED, FindLaw.)
- **Cultivation centers:** "A cultivation center agent must keep his or her ID card visible at all times when on the property of a cultivation center." (8 IAC 1300.165(g), V (LII), NEEDS VERIFICATION.)

### Oklahoma (OK), medical only
- **Regulator:** Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority (OMMA). https://oklahoma.gov/omma.html (V)
- **Rules:** https://oklahoma.gov/omma/rules-and-legislation/rules.html. Emergency rules took effect 2026-08-28 (V). The "HB3143" attribution is UNVERIFIED. The OAC 442:10 quotes below are from OMMA's "Emergency Rules Effective August 28, 2026" PDF, marked CURRENT RULES on the rules page, checked 2026-11-11 (V): https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/omma/content/rulemaking-process/rules/2026-8-28/August%2028%202026%20Emergency%20Rules.pdf
- **Pending change:** proposed permanent rules (Chapters 1 and 10) are open for comment from 2026-11-02 to 2026-12-08, with a hearing on 2026-12-08 (V, https://oklahoma.gov/omma/rules-and-legislation/public-comment.html). Re-check the cited sections after adoption.
- **Traceability:** Metrc (V). https://oklahoma.gov/omma/businesses/seed-to-sale.html
- **Licensee lookup:** https://oklahoma.gov/omma/businesses/omma-verify.html

#### Track and trace
- **OAC 442:10-5-6(d), (f)(1):** report "accurately and in real time or after each individual sale". Track, update and report "after each individual transaction, transfer, or sale".

#### Reconciliation and discrepancies
- **OAC 442:10-5-6(f)(2), daily reconciliation:** "All commercial licensees must ensure all on-premises and in-transit medical marijuana and medical marijuana product inventories are reconciled each day in the State inventory tracking system at the close of business, if not already done."
- **No significant-discrepancy threshold.** OK rules define no percentage "significant discrepancy" threshold. Don't import another state's 2% or 5%.

#### Notifications
- **OAC 442:10-5-13, loss and theft: OMMA and law enforcement immediately, no later than 24 hours, by signed statement:** "the commercial licensee shall notify immediately the Authority and law enforcement", by an electronically submitted signed statement. "The notice shall be provided no later than twenty-four hours after discovery of the event."
- **OAC 442:10-5-6(f)(3)(B), tags: OMMA and the tracking-system vendor within 48 hours:** "If a commercial licensee is unable to account for unused inventory tracking system tags, the commercial licensee must report to the Authority and the State inventory tracking system vendor within forty-eight (48) hours."
- **OAC 442:10-5-6(a), monthly report:** due by the 15th. Submitting through Metrc satisfies it (5-6(a)(4)).

#### Adjustments and corrections
- Chapter 10 sets no adjustment-reason list or correction deadline. Get reasons from the operator's Metrc account.
- **OAC 442:10-5-6(h), outage:** activity during the outage "must be immediately entered". If the outage is within the licensee's control, it "may not perform any business activities" that must be reported until access is restored.

#### Transfers, manifests and testing
- **OAC 442:10-3-6, manifests:** contents are listed in (b)(1)–(8). "A separate inventory manifest shall be prepared for each licensee receiving". The manifest may not be altered after departure except as listed in (g). "A receiving licensee shall refuse to accept any medical marijuana or medical marijuana products that are not accompanied by an inventory manifest."
- **OAC 442:10-8-1(d), testing:** "Growers, processors, and dispensaries shall not sell or otherwise transfer any final harvest batch or final product to a dispensary until the product has undergone final product testing."

#### Records retention
- "all records shall be maintained for at least seven (7) years from the date of creation" (5-6(b)); manifests also 7 years (3-6(f)).

#### Waste and destruction
- **OAC 442:10-9-9(a), 90-day transfer deadline:** "Commercial licensees shall transfer medical marijuana waste to a medical marijuana waste facility for disposal within ninety (90) days."
- **OAC 442:10-5-6(f)(8):** "All packages of medical marijuana waste shall have an inventory tracking system tag affixed and the contents of the waste package shall be reported in the State inventory tracking system."
- **OAC 442:10-5-10(b):** on-site disposal of root balls, stems and similar plant parts requires a signed disposal log, kept 7 years.

#### Worker credentials
- **OAC 442:10-5-1.1:** "A medical marijuana business license holder shall require all individuals employed under their license to have an active, unexpired credential prior to employment". The credential is "to be worn or displayed on their person during the employee's hours of work."
- **Transporters, 442:10-3-5(b):** the agent "shall carry the transporter agent license" at all times during transportation.
