# Inspection areas: what inspectors commonly look at

**Read this first.** This is an operational scaffold drawn from what cannabis inspections generally examine. It contains **no legal requirements and no citations**. Every item is "operational best practice: confirm against your state's rules." Replace or tighten items with cited rules from `state-profiles.md` or with a regulator-published checklist when one is available. Drop areas that don't apply to the license type.

Legend for applicability: C = cultivation, M = manufacturing/processing, D = distribution/transport, R = retail, All.

## 1. Licenses and premises (All)
- Current state license(s) posted or available as required; local permit if applicable
- Licensed premises boundaries match the approved floor plan / diagram; no unapproved changes to layout, rooms, or use
- Ownership/management changes reported as required (NEEDS VERIFICATION per state)
- Limited-access areas identified and controlled

## 2. Traceability records (All)
- Physical inventory reconciles with the traceability system (latest reconciliation and how variances were resolved)
- Every package/plant that requires a tag has one, and tags match the system
- Adjustments documented with reasons and supporting records
- Transfers received and sent recorded accurately and promptly
- Who has system access; credentials not shared; access removed for departed staff

## 3. Inventory controls (All)
- Storage organized so a package can be found by tag
- Quarantine/hold area for failed, pending, returned, or recalled product, clearly segregated and labeled
- Cycle count schedule and evidence of counts
- Samples (QA, lab, vendor/trade) recorded

## 4. Security and surveillance (All)
- Cameras cover required areas; recording working; retention period met (period is state-specific, NEEDS VERIFICATION)
- Ability to export or produce footage on request; who knows how
- Alarm system functional; monitoring contract current; alarm and incident logs
- Visitor log; visitor badging/escort practice
- Key/access-card control; after-hours access records

## 5. Waste and destruction (C, M, R)
- Waste log complete: date, item, weight, method, reason, people involved
- Waste rendered/destroyed and stored per state method (method, witness, notice and hold rules are state-specific: for pilot states see **Waste and destruction** in `state-profiles.md`, otherwise NEEDS VERIFICATION)
- Waste weights tie to traceability records

## 6. Cultivation-specific (C)
- Plant tagging/batching consistent with the system; plant counts match
- Canopy within licensed size; growth phases recorded
- Pesticide/nutrient application records; only permitted products used (permitted list is state-specific)
- Harvest records: wet weight, dry weight, waste, timing

## 7. Manufacturing-specific (M)
- Batch/production records: inputs (with tags), outputs, yields, dates, operator
- Equipment cleaning and maintenance logs; solvent/extraction safety documentation where applicable
- Recipes/formulations and their approval
- Sanitation, food-safety, and allergen practice for edibles (requirements vary)

## 8. Testing and COAs (All except pure transport)
- COA on file for every package that requires one, matching batch/package
- No failed, pending, or untested product in saleable inventory
- Remediation/retest records where applicable
- Sampling recorded in the traceability system

## 9. Packaging and labeling (M, D, R)
- Labels on hand match current approved formats; required warnings and content
- Child-resistant/opaque/tamper-evident packaging where required
- Label claims (potency, weight) consistent with COA

## 10. Transport (D, and anyone who transports)
- Manifests/transfer records complete and retained (retention periods are state-specific: for pilot states see **Records retention** in `state-profiles.md`)
- Vehicles and drivers meet state requirements (NEEDS VERIFICATION)
- Product secured during transport; trip records

## 11. Retail floor (R)
- Age/ID and medical-card verification procedure and evidence
- Purchase limits enforced in the POS (limits are state-specific)
- Display/sampling rules followed; no product handled outside permitted areas
- Returns and recalls handled through the system

## 12. People and training (All)
- Employee roster matches badged/registered staff; badges current (badge/registration rules state-specific: for pilot states see **Worker credentials** in `state-profiles.md`)
- Required training completed and documented
- Background-check records where required

## 13. SOPs (All)
- Written SOPs exist for each regulated activity performed
- SOPs match actual practice; staff can describe them
- Version and date on each SOP; acknowledgment of training on current version

For SOP drafting, use `cannabis-sop-generator-trainer` if it's installed.

## Walkthrough conduct (share with the team)
- Designate one escort/lead and one records runner. Others answer only questions about their own work.
- Answer what's asked, accurately. "I don't know, let me get the person who does" beats a guess.
- Never alter, discard, or backdate a record before or during an inspection.
- Note every document provided and every observation the inspector makes, for follow-up.
- Ask the inspector to clarify any finding before they leave, and how to respond.
