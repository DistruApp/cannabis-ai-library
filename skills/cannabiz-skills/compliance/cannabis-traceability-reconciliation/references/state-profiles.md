# State profiles

**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-16. **Review again by:** 2027-01-16, or sooner if a state announces a rule or system change. Colorado's revised rules took effect 2026-11-01, so the CO rule quotes below are marked NEEDS VERIFICATION until they're checked against that version.

How to read this file:
- **V** = confirmed on an official regulator/legislature page on the review date.
- **V\*** = quote confirmed directly by the library maintainer, not only by the research pass.
- **Vendor-only** = confirmed only on the vendor's own site.
- **UNVERIFIED** = don't rely on it. Ask the operator or check the source.

A rule listed here is a **pointer, not legal advice**. Always give the citation and tell the operator to confirm the current text, especially before submitting anything.

## Pilot states (deep coverage)

### California (CA)
- **Regulator:** Department of Cannabis Control (DCC). https://cannabis.ca.gov/ (V)
- **Rules:** 4 CCR Division 19. https://www.cannabis.ca.gov/cannabis-laws/dcc-regulations/ The DCC's text page is an *unofficial* compilation "in effect as of October 1, 2026". The official text is in the CCR on Westlaw.
- **Traceability:** Metrc (California Cannabis Track-and-Trace, CCTT) (V). Source: https://cannabis.ca.gov/licensees/cannaconnect-compliance-hub/
- **Licensee lookup:** https://search.cannabis.ca.gov (linked from DCC; not opened)
- **Rules relevant to Wave 1 skills** (quotes confirmed V\* on 2026-10-16 from the DCC compilation):
  - **§15049(b), Track and trace reporting:** "Each of the following activities shall be recorded in the track and trace system within 24 hours of occurrence". The listed activities include receipt, rejection of transfers, manufacturing, internal QC/R&D use, destruction or disposal, packaging/repackaging (cultivators: see §15049.1(b)(5)), lab testing, and sale or donation.
  - **§15049(e):** for package adjustments, "the licensee shall include a description explaining the reason for adjustment."
  - **§15049.1:** certain cultivation activities must be recorded "within three (3) calendar days of occurrence". See the section for the list.
  - **§15051, Reconciliation:** review the track and trace records "at least once every 30 calendar days", including "Reconciling on-hand inventory … with the records in the track and trace system". If a discrepancy is found, "the licensee shall conduct an audit and notify the Department in writing if the discrepancy is significant as defined in section 15034."
  - **§15034, Significant discrepancy:** "a five percent difference between the licensee's physical inventory and the inventory recorded in the track and trace system." *The text doesn't say whether 5% is measured per package, per item, or on total inventory, or by weight or by count. Present both readings and have compliance or counsel decide. Don't pick one.*
  - **§15048.1, Account manager:** "Correct any data entry errors within three (3) calendar days of discovery". Reconcile "at least once every thirty (30) calendar days."
  - **§17223, Waste:** a written cannabis waste management plan is required (quoted by the research pass, V).
- **Pending change:** DCC rulemaking DCC-2026-02-R may amend these sections. Text and status are UNVERIFIED.

### Colorado (CO)
- **Regulator:** Marijuana Enforcement Division (MED). https://med.colorado.gov/ (V)
- **Rules:** 1 CCR 212-3. https://med.colorado.gov/rules. Revised rules adopted 2026-09-10, **effective 2026-11-01**.
- **Traceability:** Metrc, "required to use METRC as the primary inventory tracking system of record" (V). Source: https://med.colorado.gov/metrc
- **Licensee lookup:** https://med.colorado.gov/licensee-information-and-lookup-tool (V)
- **Rules relevant to Wave 1:**
  - **Rule 3-805(E)(1):** "A Licensee must reconcile all on-premises and in-transit Regulated Marijuana inventories each day in the Inventory Tracking System at the close of business." **NEEDS VERIFICATION** against the rules effective 2026-11-01. (Quoted from the version effective 2026-01-05; the same wording appears in the Secretary of State's 1 CCR 212-3 text as retrieved 2026-11-10, but which version that text is wasn't confirmed.)
  - **Rule 3-805(E)(4):** "Secondary software applications must use the Inventory Tracking System data as the primary source of data." This matters for ERP/POS: Metrc is the record of truth. **NEEDS VERIFICATION**, same as above.
  - To confirm, use the 2026-11-01 redlines and adoption order linked from https://med.colorado.gov/rules.

### Michigan (MI)
- **Regulator:** Cannabis Regulatory Agency (CRA). https://www.michigan.gov/cra. The site blocked automated access, so it's UNVERIFIED by fetch. Check in a browser.
- **Traceability:** Metrc, the "statewide monitoring system" (Vendor-only: https://www.metrc.com/partner/michigan/). Latest MI Metrc bulletin seen: Bulletin 118 (2026-10-14).
- **Rules:** Mich. Admin. Code R 420.210 (secondary source, Cornell LII, eff. 2022-03-07): "A licensee shall not transfer or sell a marihuana product that is not identified in the statewide monitoring system … A licensee shall immediately tag, identify, or record as part of a batch…". A CRA rules rewrite is pending, and whether it's been adopted is UNVERIFIED. **Treat every MI timing requirement as NEEDS VERIFICATION.**

### Massachusetts (MA)
- **Regulator:** Cannabis Control Commission (CCC). https://masscannabiscontrol.com/ (V)
- **Rules:** 935 CMR 500 (adult use) and 501 (medical), listed as effective 2026-09-11. https://masscannabiscontrol.com/regulations/
- **Traceability:** Metrc (V). https://masscannabiscontrol.com/metrc/
- **Licensee lookup:** https://masscannabiscontrol.com/licensing-tracker/
- **Rules relevant to Wave 1:** 935 CMR 500.105(8) (from the CCC PDF dated 2026-04-24): "Real-time inventory shall be maintained … Conduct a monthly inventory … Conduct a comprehensive annual inventory", and "failure to enter inventory into the Seed-to-sale SOR may result in the suspension or revocation". Confirm against the version effective 2026-09-11.

### New York (NY)
- **Regulator:** Office of Cannabis Management (OCM). https://cannabis.ny.gov/ (V)
- **Rules:** 9 NYCRR Parts 113–133 (linked from OCM).
- **Traceability:** **Metrc, which replaced BioTrack NY** (V). The integration deadline for all licensees was 2025-12-17. https://cannabis.ny.gov/seed-to-sale. Later dates on that page (retail package-UID linkage, Retail Item IDs, C.O.D. reporting moving to Metrc) were paraphrased, not quoted, so they're UNVERIFIED.
- **Licensee lookup:** https://data.ny.gov/Economic-Development/Current-OCM-Licenses/jskf-tt3q
- **Gotcha:** data and SOPs from the BioTrack era are out of date. Don't apply BioTrack workflows to NY.

### Illinois (IL)
- **Regulators:** split by license type. IDFPR (dispensaries); IDOA (cultivation centers, craft growers, infusers, transporters); IDPH (medical patients). https://cannabis.illinois.gov/ (V)
- **Traceability:** **Metrc, which replaced BioTrack** (V). IDOA Informational Bulletin IB-2025-03: "July 1: Metrc becomes the official cannabis inventory tracking system for Illinois." State updates after that date describe licensees operating in Metrc. https://cannabis.illinois.gov/research-and-data/seed-to-sale-tracking.html
- **Licensee lookup:** dispensaries at https://cannabis.illinois.gov/about/locations.html
- **Gotcha:** confirm which agency regulates the operator's license type before citing any rule.

### Oklahoma (OK), medical only
- **Regulator:** Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority (OMMA). https://oklahoma.gov/omma.html (V)
- **Rules:** https://oklahoma.gov/omma/rules-and-legislation/rules.html. Emergency rules took effect 2026-08-28 (HB3143).
- **Traceability:** Metrc (V). https://oklahoma.gov/omma/businesses/seed-to-sale.html
- **Licensee lookup:** https://oklahoma.gov/omma/businesses/omma-verify.html

## Metrc documentation
- State API docs: `https://api-{state}.metrc.com/Documentation` (e.g. api-ca). `api-docs.metrc.com` does **not** resolve.
- Bulletins: state guidance comes out as numbered Metrc bulletins. Archive: https://www.metrc.com/information-and-materials/bulletin-archive (filter by state).
- Adjustment reasons: the API lists `GET /packages/v2/adjust/reasons`. Get the state's list from the operator's Metrc account; never assume it.
